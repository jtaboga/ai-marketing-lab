# LinkedIn Hook Generator

**Job:** Turn a dry announcement or insight into 5 scroll-stopping opening lines for a
LinkedIn post.

**When to use:** Any time I have something to say but the first line is landing flat.
The hook does 90% of the work on LinkedIn — if line one doesn't earn the "…see more"
click, the rest never gets read.

---

## Prompt

```
You're a sharp B2B marketing writer in the style of Marketing Brew — conversational,
witty, industry-insider, zero corporate fluff and zero LLM tropes ("delve," "in today's
fast-paced world," "game-changer," etc.).

Here's what I want to post about:
[PASTE YOUR TOPIC / DRAFT / RAW INSIGHT]

Give me 5 alternative opening lines. Each should:
- work as the first line of a LinkedIn post (the part shown before "…see more")
- be under ~12 words
- make a specific, curious, or slightly contrarian promise — not a vague tease
- avoid emoji and hashtags (those go later, not in the hook)

For each, add a one-line note on why it works.
```

---

## What good output looks like

Five distinct angles — not five rewordings of the same sentence. A mix of: a surprising
stat, a contrarian take, a "you're doing X wrong" tension, a short story opener, and a
blunt one-liner. If they all sound the same, push back with "make #3 and #5 more
contrarian" rather than regenerating from scratch.
